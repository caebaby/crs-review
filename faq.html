How long does a CRS shoe last?

Dependent upon environmental factors. Expect to replace shoes more frequently in drier, rocky climates. Softer, wet environments can reset shoes for several cycles.

Can I apply the shoe myself?

CRS Horseshoes application is extremely easy! We recommend finding a hoof care professional that can guarantee the product is applied properly and for your horse to reap the most benefit.

What horses are a good fit for CRS?

Horses that are a grade 5 on the lameness scale all the way through performance horses that are sound.

Does CRS work for hind shoes?

Yes, the CRS Hind shoe not only provides protection, but can also serve horses that have a negative plantar angle.

How do you apply CRS Horseshoes?

You can nail our shoes on using the pre-selected nail holes found on each shoe (MX 50, 60, 70 recommended). You can glue the shoe on using an adhesive or acrylic glue. You can screw our shoes on for horses that object to the driving of nails — specifically laminitis and founder cases.
