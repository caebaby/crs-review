Putting CRS on the horse.

Three ways to apply. Your farrier picks the one that suits the horse.

01

Nail on

Use the pre-selected nail holes found on each shoe. MX 50, 60, 70 recommended.

02

Glue on

Apply with adhesive or acrylic glue.

03

Screw on

For horses that object to the driving of nails, specifically laminitis and founder cases.
