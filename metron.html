Metron FAQ.

How does my horse benefit from Metron imaging?
A Metron report helps us see mechanical concerns like a low palmar angle or misalignment, and quickly work to resolve the issues. A Metron report is an important baseline from which we can develop a sustainable plan for your horse, and track progress.
Can you use my existing X-rays?
Yes — Metron works on X-rays or photographs. If you have recent X-rays from your vet, send them along. If not, we’ll walk you through taking photos that work.
Is this a veterinary diagnosis?
No. Metron reports are not intended to be diagnostic — they are used only to assess mechanics. If you have a clinical concern, your vet remains the right first call. A Metron report can give your vet useful mechanical context alongside their examination.
