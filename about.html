Our story

It started with horses no one could fix.

After 30 years of using traditional methods, I was no closer to helping the horses that needed help the most. Out of sheer desperation, I started forging unusual shapes in steel shoes.

The horses began to respond favorably. This prompted the idea of CRS horseshoes.

Mission

Every horse deserves a second chance.

CRS Horseshoes develops innovative hoof care solutions designed to support long-term soundness, comfort, and performance in every horse.

The patent

US 11,026,413 — granted 2020.

In 2020 we received a patent for a horseshoe with the unique feature of a rounded medial/lateral plane. The mechanical advantage resolves leverage that can’t be addressed with a flat shoe. There is no one plane that’s correct all the time.
